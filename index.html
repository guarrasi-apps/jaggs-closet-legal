Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 September 2026 · JAGGs Closet, by JAGGs Boutique LLC (Mount Pocono, Pennsylvania, USA)

JAGGs Closet is a wardrobe app. You photograph your clothes, the app cuts out the background and keeps a catalogue of your pieces and outfits. Everything you put in it stays on your phone.

What the app stores, and where

Pieces, looks, tags, measurements and every photo you add live in the app's own folder on your device. They are included in your iPhone's normal iCloud or computer backup if you have that switched on, because that is how iOS backs up apps. You can export a backup file yourself from the Me tab and save it wherever you like.

Delete a piece and its photos are deleted with it. Delete the app and everything in it is removed from the phone.

What leaves your phone

Three things, none of which involve your photos or your closet:

  • App updates. The app asks Expo's update service (Expo Application Services, u.expo.dev) whether a newer version exists. That request includes your IP address, the app version, and the platform — the same technical details any software update check sends. No photos, nothing from your closet.
  • Smart detection files. The first time you add a photo, the app downloads Google's MediaPipe library (from the jsDelivr CDN) and two small model files (from Google's storage servers). These are plain file downloads. The models then run on your phone; your photo is never sent anywhere.
  • TestFlight, during beta testing. While the app is distributed through Apple TestFlight, Apple collects crash reports, basic device and usage information, and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send. That is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy and the TestFlight terms, and it is how we receive bug reports.

There are no other network connections. Fonts, icons and the app itself are bundled inside the app, so it works with no internet connection at all once smart detection has its files.

Camera and photo access

The app asks for camera access so you can photograph a piece, and photo library access so you can pick photos you already have. It only uses the photos you choose, and it works on them on the device.

People in your photos

The app can find the outline of a person and their clothing in a photo so it can remove the background or cut out just the clothes. It does not identify anyone. It does not create, store or compare faceprints, face geometry, or any other biometric identifier, and it does not perform facial recognition. When you set your colouring, the app averages the colour of skin in your model photo and stores that single colour value on your phone.

If you photograph other people, please ask them first — and again before you post or list a photo of them anywhere.

Children

JAGGs Closet is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect anything from them. There is nothing for us to collect from anyone, but please don't let under-13s use it.

Your rights

Because your data never reaches us, requests to access, correct, export or delete "your data on our servers" have no subject matter — there is no server. You can export everything yourself (Me → Export backup) and delete everything yourself (delete a piece, or delete the app). If you are in a place with privacy laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA and you believe we hold something about you, write to us using the contact details below and we will answer.
