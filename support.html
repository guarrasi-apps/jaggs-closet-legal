Getting help or reporting a bug

The fastest way during the beta: take a screenshot inside JAGGs Closet, then tap Share Beta Feedback. It reaches us with the screenshot and your note. You can also send feedback from the TestFlight app under the app's name.

Things worth knowing

  • Photos are processed on your phone. The first photo you add downloads the detection files (about 16 MB) — do that one on Wi-Fi.
  • A photo with a person in it becomes a model shot: the person is kept, the background removed. A photo of clothes on their own is cut out piece by piece.
  • Anything the app gets wrong can be fixed by hand: Fix it → Box, Erase or Restore.
  • Your closet is stored on the phone. Export a backup (Me → Export backup) before you delete the app or change phones.
  • TestFlight builds expire after 90 days; install the newer build when it arrives and your closet carries over.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. Short version: your photos and closet never leave your phone.
